What exactly am I holding?

You hold $GTAVI (Grab That Ass), a community token on Solana. Verify its full contract address in Launch Status before trading. Bitcoin rewards and your $GTAVI holdings are separate assets.

How does holding turn into Bitcoin?

The described rewards model allocates a portion of trading fees to eligible holders. Distributions depend on trading activity and the project’s payout rules. Rewards are variable and are not guaranteed. The site’s payout data source is currently awaiting connection.

Is the reward really BTC?

The rewards described on this site use wrapped Bitcoin (WBTC) on Solana. Verify the reward token’s mint and each payout transaction. Verified amounts and history will appear in the rewards checker when its data source is connected.

Do I need to connect my wallet or claim here?

You can enter your public Solana wallet address to use the read-only rewards interface. This site requires no wallet connection, transaction signature, or claim payment. Never share your seed phrase or private keys.
